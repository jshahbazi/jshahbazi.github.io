The other day I wrote about testing TypeSafe's Jev as a replacement for our Claude-based answer grader (https://jshahbazi.github.io/evaluating-jev-as-an-eval-grader). Since then a handful of open-source lookalikes have appeared, so we tested six of them (Jeff, Jeeves, Kev, Decider, and two sizes of JevK5) as answer graders on 100 hand-graded cases to see whether any could do the job on a machine we own. JevK5-4B agreed with our human graders 89% of the time against Jev's 90%, returned a decision in roughly 160ms on a Mac, and let 8 of the 33 bad answers through where the next-best open model, Jeeves, let 14 through. Its confidence score isn't yet reliable enough to decide which cases stay local, so our production grader hasn't changed, but it's the open model we'd try first. Full writeup, with the tables and caveats: https://jshahbazi.github.io/testing-jevk5-4b-as-a-free-local-jev-alternative
